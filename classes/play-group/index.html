What age should my child be for Play Group?

Play Group is for children aged 2–3 years. Your child should be at least the lower age by the start of the school year. Not sure which class fits? Call +91 73977 73618 and we will help you decide.

What are the Play Group school timings?

Play Group runs from 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM. Our GPS-tracked school vans cover Nagercoil, Colachel, Thuckalay, Padmanabhapuram and Marthandam, so children can travel with trained women attendants.

How big are the Play Group classes?

We keep Play Group small: 1 teacher per 8 children, plus a dedicated woman care attendant in every classroom to help with personal care and hygiene.

What will my child learn in Play Group?

A gentle first step from home to school. Days are built around sensory play, rhymes and songs, and getting comfortable in a small, caring group. Highlights include: sensory bins, water play and texture discovery; action songs, rhymes and first words; sharing, simple routines and gentle toilet training; big crayons, dough and stacking for little fingers.

How do I apply for Play Group admission for 2026–2027?

Send an enquiry from our admission page (it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in) or call +91 73977 73618. Our admissions team replies with fees and available seats, usually within a day, and invites you to visit the campus with your child.
