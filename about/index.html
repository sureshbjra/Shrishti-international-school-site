Why Parents Choose Us

A Joyful, Safe Start Before Big School

Children leave UKG reading, writing and confident, ready for CBSE, ICSE or State Board primary schools. Here is how we get them there.

  • International early-years practice

    British EYFS and Canadian inquiry-based methods, with guidance from IIT alumni on early maths, logic and learning systems.

  • Teachers who understand little ones

    Teachers trained in child psychology who know every child by name, so learning never feels like pressure.

  • Small classes, extra hands

    1 teacher per 8 children in Play Group, 10 in Pre KG and 12 in LKG and UKG, plus a woman care attendant in every classroom.

  • Reading early, with joy

    Jolly Phonics, stories and interactive smart boards. By the end of UKG, children read short stories and write neatly.

  • Healthy habits from day one

    RO-filtered drinking water, clean dining spaces, guided hand-washing and healthy eating at snack time.

  • Safe journeys to school

    Air-conditioned, GPS-tracked vans with speed governors and trained women attendants.
