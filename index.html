Spaces built for small hands and big curiosity

Every room is planned around how young children learn: by touching, moving, making and asking questions, always in sight of a caring adult.

  • Smart classrooms

    Interactive displays for phonics and story time, with child-sized furniture that keeps little backs comfortable.

  • Art and music studio

    Painting, clay, puppet theatre and music and movement, with room to make a mess.

  • Safe outdoor play

    Soft rubber flooring, child-sized climbing frames, tunnels and balance tracks for strong, confident bodies.

  • Healthy meals and hygiene

    RO-filtered drinking water, clean dining spaces, guided hand-washing and healthy eating habits from day one.

  • GPS-tracked school vans

    Air-conditioned vans with speed governors and trained women attendants, covering Nagercoil, Marthandam, Colachel, Thuckalay and Padmanabhapuram.

  • Discovery corner

    Science kits, magnetic building blocks and beginner robotics puzzles that turn questions into experiments.

A day at Shrishti Kinder

A steady rhythm that young children can rely on: focused learning in the fresh morning hours, time to run and play, then calm, creative afternoons. Timings shown are for LKG and UKG.

  1. 8:30am

    Morning circle

    A warm welcome from teachers, rhymes, sharing how we feel, and assembly.

  2. 9:15am

    Phonics, numbers and discovery

    Jolly Phonics, counting blocks and smart-board lessons, all hands-on.

  3. 10:45am

    Snack time

    Hand-washing, healthy food, table manners and chatting with friends.

  4. 11:30am

    Outdoor play

    Climbing frames, balance beams, the sand pit and ball games.

  5. 12:30pm

    Art and music

    Painting, clay, dress-up, instruments and puppet shows.

  6. 1:30pm

    Story and home time

    A story to talk about, a quiet look back at the day, and a safe handover to parents or the van.

Which is the best kindergarten school in Kanyakumari district?

Shrishti Kinder International School in Nagercoil is widely recognised as the best kindergarten and international preschool in Kanyakumari district. Backed by the Jeevanantham Educational Trust and guided by UK and Canadian consultants and IIT alumni, we offer experienced teachers, smart classrooms, sensory play spaces and a safe, secure campus.

Is admission open for the 2026–2027 academic year?

Yes. Admissions are open for Play Group (2–3 years), Pre KG (3–4 years), LKG (4–5 years) and UKG (5–6 years). Classes are kept small, so seats are limited. You can apply online or call +91 73977 73618.

Where is the school?

Our campus is in Kattaikadu, Ammandivillai, Nagercoil – 629 204, Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu. It is easy to reach from Nagercoil town, Marthandam, Colachel, Padmanabhapuram and nearby areas.

What makes Shrishti Kinder different from other preschools?

We combine British and Canadian inquiry-based early-years practice with teachers trained in child psychology, hands-on sensory learning, interactive smart boards, healthy food habits and GPS-tracked transport. Children don't just memorise: they build confidence, early reading and creative thinking.

How many children are there for each teacher?

Play Group has 1 teacher for every 8 children. Pre KG has 1 for every 10, and LKG and UKG have 1 for every 12. Every classroom also has a dedicated woman care attendant to help with personal care and hygiene.

Do you provide school transport?

Yes. Our air-conditioned, GPS-tracked vans have speed governors and trained women attendants. They cover Nagercoil, Marthandam, Colachel, Thuckalay and surrounding areas.

Come and see us

The best way to choose a school is to visit. Walk through the classrooms, meet the teachers and let your child try the play area.
