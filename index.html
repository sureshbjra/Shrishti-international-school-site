Our Facilities

Spaces Built for Small Hands and Big Curiosity

Every room is planned around how young children learn: by touching, moving, making and asking questions, always in sight of a caring adult.

  • Smart classrooms

    Interactive displays for phonics and story time, with child-sized furniture that keeps little backs comfortable.

  • Art and music studio

    Painting, clay, puppet theatre and music and movement, with room to make a mess.

  • Safe outdoor play

    Soft rubber flooring, child-sized climbing frames, tunnels and balance tracks for strong, confident bodies.

  • Healthy meals and hygiene

    RO-filtered drinking water, clean dining spaces, guided hand-washing and healthy eating habits from day one.

  • GPS-tracked school vans

    Air-conditioned vans with speed governors and trained women attendants, covering Nagercoil, Marthandam, Colachel, Thuckalay and Padmanabhapuram.

  • Discovery corner

    Science kits, magnetic building blocks and beginner robotics puzzles that turn questions into experiments.

A Day Here

    Painting, clay, dress-up, instruments and puppet shows.

  6. 1:30pm

    Story and home time

    A story to talk about, a quiet look back at the day, and a safe handover to parents or the van.

Which is the best kindergarten school in Kanyakumari district?

Shrishti International School in Nagercoil is widely recognised as the best kindergarten and international preschool in Kanyakumari district. Backed by the Jeevanantham Educational Trust and guided by UK and Canadian consultants and IIT alumni, we offer experienced teachers, smart classrooms, sensory play spaces and a safe, secure campus.

Is admission open for the 2026–2027 academic year?

Yes. Admissions are open for Play Group (2–3 years), Pre KG (3–4 years), LKG (4–5 years) and UKG (5–6 years). Classes are kept small, so seats are limited. You can apply online or call +91 73977 73618.

What is the right age for Play Group, Pre KG, LKG and UKG admission?

Play Group: 2–3 years; Pre KG: 3–4 years; LKG: 4–5 years; UKG: 5–6 years. Your child should be at least the lower age by the start of the school year. If you're unsure which class fits, call us and we'll help you decide.

What will my child learn at Shrishti International School?

Early reading with Jolly Phonics, numbers and logic games, art and craft, music and dance, yoga and outdoor play, and hands-on science in our smart classrooms. By the end of UKG, children read short stories, write neatly and are ready for CBSE, ICSE or State Board primary schools.

Where is the school?

Our campus is at Kattaikadu, Ammandivilai, Nagercoil – 629 204 (Kalkulam taluk), Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu, between Colachel and Nagercoil. It is easy to reach from Muttom, Vellichanthai, Kadiapattinam, Manavalakurichi, Colachel, Thuckalay and Nagercoil town.

Is there a good play school near Colachel, Muttom or Manavalakurichi?

Yes. Shrishti International School in Ammandivilai is close to Kattaikadu, Kalpadi, Kadiapattinam, Muttom, Vellichanthai, Manavalakurichi, Mandaikadu, Kurumpanai, Colachel. Children from these areas join our Play Group, Pre KG, LKG and UKG classes, and our school vans run through Colachel.

What makes Shrishti International School different from other preschools?

We combine British and Canadian inquiry-based early-years practice with teachers trained in child psychology, hands-on sensory learning, interactive smart boards, healthy food habits and GPS-tracked transport. Children don't just memorise: they build confidence, early reading and creative thinking.

How many children are there for each teacher?

Play Group has 1 teacher for every 8 children. Pre KG has 1 for every 10, and LKG and UKG have 1 for every 12. Every classroom also has a dedicated woman care attendant to help with personal care and hygiene.

Which areas does the school van cover?

Our air-conditioned, GPS-tracked vans have speed governors and trained women attendants. They cover Nagercoil, Colachel, Thuckalay, Padmanabhapuram, Marthandam and surrounding areas. Live somewhere else, such as Eraniel, Thingal Nagar or Vadasery? Call or WhatsApp us and we'll tell you the nearest stop.
